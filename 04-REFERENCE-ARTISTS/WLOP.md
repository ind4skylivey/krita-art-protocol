# Artist Profile: WLOP (Wang Ling)

## 🎨 Signature Style
- **Atmospheric Perspective:** Uses fog, light shafts, and particles to create immense depth.
- **Dramatic Contrast:** Strong focus on the relationship between extreme highlights and deep shadows.
- **Textured Brushes:** Unlike clean cel-shading, WLOP uses brushes that leave "noise" and texture, giving it a traditional feel.

## 🛠️ Techniques to Study
1. **Light Shafts:** Use the `Airbrush Soft` in `Color Dodge` mode to create light rays.
2. **Subsurface Scattering:** Look for the "glow" on the edges of ears or skin when backlit.
3. **Simplified Detail:** He suggests detail in the shadows rather than painting every pixel.

## 📅 Roadmap Application
- **Days 30-45:** Focus on his lighting during the Specialization phase.
- **Day 55:** Attempt an atmospheric background inspired by his "GhostBlade" series.
