# Artist Profile: Redum

## 🎨 Signature Style
- **Porcelain Skin:** Achieves a flawless, almost "glowing" skin texture that looks both soft and solid.
- **Master of SSS:** Uses vibrant oranges and reds at the edges of shadows to simulate light passing through skin (Subsurface Scattering).
- **Intricate Eye Detail:** Renders eyes with multiple layers of depth, making them look like polished gemstones.
- **Harmonious Palettes:** Often uses a primary color theme with subtle, sophisticated variations in the shadow tones.

## 🛠️ Techniques to Study
1. **The "Glow" Effect:** Notice how the transition between light and shadow isn't just a darker color, but often a *more saturated* one.
2. **Hair Volume:** Study how Redum treats hair as large, flowing shapes first, only adding individual "hero" strands at the very end.
3. **Soft Occlusion:** Unlike aggressive black shadows, Redum uses deep, saturated hues for occlusion zones (e.g., deep purples or warm browns).

## 📅 Roadmap Application
- **Days 30-40:** Study Redum's skin rendering during the "Advanced Shadows" phase.
- **Day 50:** Master study focus: Try to replicate the "glow" on a portrait's cheek or nose using Redum’s saturation logic.
