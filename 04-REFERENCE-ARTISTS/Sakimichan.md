# Artist Profile: Sakimichan

## 🎨 Signature Style
- **Realistic Rendering:** Known for extremely smooth skin and high-fidelity textures.
- **Lighting Dynamics:** Uses rim lights and bounced light to make characters "pop" from the canvas.
- **Form Mastery:** Focuses on the flow of the body and muscle definition under light.

## 🛠️ Techniques to Study
1. **Skin Rendering:** Notice the use of pinks and reds in transition areas (Shadow 2 in our system).
2. **Eye Highlights:** Study how she renders eyes like glass, with multiple layers of reflection.
3. **Hard/Soft Blend:** She uses the airbrush for large forms but keeps clothing edges razor-sharp.

## 📅 Roadmap Application
- **Days 40-50:** Study her skin rendering techniques during the Specialization phase.
