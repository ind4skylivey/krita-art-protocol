# Artist Profile: Tarou

## 🎨 Signature Style
- **Clean Lineart:** Extremely consistent line weight and sharp closures.
- **Flat Shading:** Uses very few colors but places them perfectly to imply volume.
- **Graphic Composition:** Focuses on the silhouette and negative space.

## 🛠️ Techniques to Study
1. **Silhouette Control:** Look at how the character's shape is readable even as a black blob.
2. **Minimal Shadows:** He often uses only ONE shadow color. Study where he places it.
3. **Bold Colors:** Uses high-contrast color schemes that draw the eye immediately.

## 📅 Roadmap Application
- **Weeks 1-2:** Perfect reference for your initial lineart and basic shading exercises.
