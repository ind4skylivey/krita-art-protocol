# Artist Profile: Ilya Kuvshinov

## 🎨 Signature Style
- **Color Transitions:** Master of shifting from hard cel-shading to soft gradients.
- **Cinematic Lighting:** Uses a "rim light" effect to separate characters from the background.
- **Simplification:** Reduces complex anatomy into appealing, bold shapes.

## 🛠️ Techniques to Study
1. **The Rim Light:** Notice how he uses a very bright, thin line on the edges of silhouettes.
2. **Soft vs. Hard:** Study where he keeps an edge sharp (nose, eyes) vs. where he blurs it (cheeks, hair shadows).
3. **Muted Palettes:** He often uses a limited color range with one high-saturation accent.

## 📅 Roadmap Application
- **Days 16-25:** Study his portrait lighting during the Advanced Shadows phase.
- **Day 40:** Try a "Master Study" of one of his simpler pieces.
